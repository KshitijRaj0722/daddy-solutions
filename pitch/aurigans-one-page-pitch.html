Daily Sales & Inventory Dashboard

Proposal for Aurigans India Private Limited · from Daddy Solutions

The problem

Aurigans runs multiple outlets across airports and campuses. Sales, stock and wastage figures sit in separate POS systems and spreadsheets. Management sees them late, and problems like stock-outs, over-ordering and unusual sales are caught after the money is lost.

What we'll build

One dashboard that gives owners and outlet managers a clear picture every morning.

FeatureWhat it does
Sales consolidationPulls sales from each outlet's POS automatically. No manual exports.
Outlet comparisonDaily and weekly sales by outlet, brand and item, with top and bottom sellers.
Inventory trackingCurrent stock per outlet with low-stock alerts and reorder suggestions.
Wastage logSimple entry for staff, with wastage cost reported by outlet.
Morning summaryAutomatic WhatsApp or email report to management at a set time.
Anomaly alertsA message when an outlet's sales or stock is out of the ordinary.
