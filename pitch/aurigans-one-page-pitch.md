# Daily Sales & Inventory Dashboard
**Proposal for Aurigans India Private Limited · from Daddy Solutions**

## The problem
Aurigans runs multiple outlets across airports and campuses. Sales, stock and wastage figures sit in separate POS systems and spreadsheets. Management sees them late, and problems like stock-outs, over-ordering and unusual sales are caught after the money is lost.

## What we'll build
One dashboard that gives owners and outlet managers a clear picture every morning.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sales consolidation** | Pulls sales from each outlet's POS automatically. No manual exports. |
| **Outlet comparison** | Daily and weekly sales by outlet, brand and item, with top and bottom sellers. |
| **Inventory tracking** | Current stock per outlet with low-stock alerts and reorder suggestions. |
| **Wastage log** | Simple entry for staff, with wastage cost reported by outlet. |
| **Morning summary** | Automatic WhatsApp or email report to management at a set time. |
| **Anomaly alerts** | A message when an outlet's sales or stock is out of the ordinary. |

## Scope
**Included:** up to [3] outlets at launch, one POS integration, one dashboard with role-based logins, daily summary report, alerts, staff training and a handover document.

**Not included (available later):** purchase-order and vendor workflows, staff rostering, customer feedback tools, additional outlets and integrations.
